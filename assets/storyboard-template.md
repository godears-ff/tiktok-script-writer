# TikTok Storyboard Template

The rows below show the 80-second default. Scale the time blocks
proportionally for a shorter script. Keep all camera, scene, and text-overlay
direction in this storyboard rather than the spoken script.

```
| Shot | Time | Camera / Angle | Visual Description | Text Overlay | Audio |
|------|------|----------------|--------------------|--------------|-------|
| 1 | 0-3s | Close-up, eye-level | Host stares at camera with exaggerated "wait what" expression. Jump cut. | "STOP SCROLLING" | Whoosh + beat start |
| 2 | 3-8s | Medium, slightly low | Host gestures with hands and establishes the context. Text flashes on key words. | "This ONE thing" / "Is DESTROYING" / "Your progress" | Upbeat background |
| 3 | 8-18s | B-roll / screen recording | Show the actual hack, example, or proof in action. Slow motion or zoom for emphasis. | [Key step or verified fact] | Beat drop at reveal |
| 4 | 18-35s | Wide / environment | Host in context (gym, office, kitchen). Show the cause, evidence, or comparison. | "Here's the truth:" | Music lowers |
| 5 | 35-55s | Close-up, direct gaze | Host delivers the twist or counter-intuitive truth. Slow zoom in. | [Bold claim or question] | Music builds |
| 6 | 55-70s | Medium / B-roll | Summarize the payoff or answer the opening question. | [Summary phrase] | Music steadies |
| 7 | 70-80s | Close-up + hand gesture | Host points down or left toward the CTA and ends cleanly. | "Follow for part 2" / "Save this" | Ending jingle |
```

## Shot Blocking Notes
- **Close-up**: 80% of video time. Builds connection, trust, and urgency.
- **Jump cuts**: Every 2–5 seconds. Remove pauses, breaths, and filler words.
- **Text overlay changes**: Every time a new idea starts. Maximum 3 words per text frame.
- **Eye contact**: Hold eye contact with the lens, not the screen. Break eye contact only for emphasis or B-roll.
- **Hand gestures**: Use exaggerated hand movements to emphasize key words.
- **Background**: Clean, well-lit, or context-relevant. No distracting clutter.
- **Evidence safety**: Never alter documents, surveillance frames, or source footage to create proof.

## Transition Ideas
- **Whoosh + zoom**: For jumping between ideas
- **Clap snap**: To signal "that's it" or a punchline
- **Freeze frame + text**: For emphasis on a key stat or reaction
- **Spin / whip pan**: For POV shifts or time jumps
- **Match cut**: Matching action or shape between two scenes

## CTA Shot Ideas
- **Point to "Follow" button area**: Physical gesture + arrow animation
- **Finger tap on "Save" icon**: Overlay animation
- **"Comment _____ below"**: Text flash with a specific word or emoji
- **Split screen**: Show "before" vs "after" with the CTA in the center
- **End card**: Static frame with channel name, series name, and follow button
- **Duet / Stitch open**: Leave 1–2 seconds of silent space at the end for others to react
