# Final Quality Scorecard

Use this scorecard for substantial scripts, sensitive topics, optimization
passes, or before committing a final publication package.

A high score does not override a critical failure.

## 100-Point Review

| Area | Points | What to check |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Hook | 15 | Specific conflict, contradiction, result, evidence, or emotional moment; question-first or contrast-led framing when the topic supports it |
| Answer axis | Included in Hook | The direction of the answer lands by 0:05 and does not overclaim the evidence |
| Viewer promise | 10 | One primary viewer and one reason to keep watching |
| Clarity | 10 | Names, sequence, legal stage, and takeaways are understandable |
| Pacing | 10 | Meaningful visual, text, audio, or emotional change every 2-3 seconds |
| Evidence density | 15 | Facts, quotes, dates, numbers, documents, or source details advance the story |
| Emotional or identity stake | 10 | The viewer understands why the story matters to them |
| Creator voice and authenticity | 10 | Sounds like the approved creator, not a generic AI or news wire |
| CTA | 5 | Decision, prediction, experience, correction, save, or share |
| Accuracy and safety | 10 | Attribution, privacy, legal limits, minors, health, and platform policy |
| Bilingual fidelity | 5 | English and Chinese preserve the same claims, uncertainty, and CTA |

## Celebrity Explainer Series Checks

When the automatic celebrity mode applies, also verify:

- The six beats are present and in order.
- The hook uses an existing supported question, not a fabricated decision.
- The answer axis lands by 0:05.
- There are three meaningful contrast layers.
- Every material claim has a source or explicit qualifier.
- Private motives, body measurements, sexual history, and intimate details are
  not treated as proof.
- The judgment is visibly framed as interpretation.
- The CTA matches the subtype rather than using a generic "What do you think?"

## Person Profile Introduction Checks

When the automatic person profile mode applies, verify:

- The script is a coherent introduction, not a list of resume bullets.
- The first three seconds use a verified retention hook and state why the
  viewer should keep watching.
- One clear career thesis connects the person's background, expertise, and
  current contribution.
- The hook uses a verified role or achievement, not inflated praise.
- Two or three turning points are selected rather than every resume item.
- Skills and impact are supported by dates, roles, projects, or measurable
  results.
- The ending looks forward and does not invent goals, motives, or private
  information.
- The structure is visibly different from the celebrity explainer series.
- A supplied URL was actually read through the available web-reading skill,
  not inferred from a title or search snippet.
- External-source facts include the source link, access date, and any
  verification limit.
- The English script and Chinese translation preserve identical facts,
  positioning, dates, and qualifiers.

## Suggested Thresholds

- 90-100: publication-ready after normal QA
- 80-89: strong draft; fix the lowest-scoring area
- 70-79: needs structural revision
- Below 70: rewrite before production

Do not treat the score as a predictive ranking metric. It is an editorial
quality check.

## Critical Fails

Any of the following requires a rewrite regardless of score:

- Unsupported allegation presented as fact
- Arrest, charge, lawsuit, or rumor described as a conviction
- Minor's private information exposed unnecessarily
- Medical, legal, or financial claim without the required limits
- Graphic death, abuse, self-harm, or sexual detail used as spectacle
- Fabricated evidence, altered document, or misleading reenactment
- Harassment, doxxing, or targeted abuse encouraged
- Copyright, sponsorship, AI, or rights disclosure missing when required
- Chinese translation changes the strength of a claim or legal qualifier
- Failed fact check or unverified quote used as the hook

## Review Output

When the user asks for a review, return:

1. Total score
2. Scores by category
3. Strongest element
4. Biggest retention or clarity problem
5. Answer-axis and contrast-matrix check when applicable
6. Critical-fail audit
7. Three concrete rewrite priorities
8. Revised hook or CTA when useful
