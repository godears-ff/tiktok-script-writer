# Creator Voice Profile

Use this reference when a creator provides samples, approved scripts,
previous captions, audience feedback, or explicit preferences. The goal is to
write in the user's approved voice, not to imitate another living creator.

## What the Profile Controls

- Primary audience
- Reading and speaking level
- Sentence length and pacing
- Signature phrases
- Banned words and claims
- Humor style
- Emotional range
- Authority and skepticism level
- Topic-specific tone shifts
- CTA style
- Caption and hashtag habits
- Visual and editing patterns

## Required Profile Fields

| Field | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| Primary viewer | Who the content is for |
| Content pillars | Recurring topics and series |
| Voice | Calm, energetic, analytical, conversational, blunt, playful, etc. |
| Point of view | Insider, reporter, skeptic, fan, analyst, storyteller |
| Sentence rhythm | Short, medium, long, mixed, staccato, conversational |
| Signature phrases | Words or structures approved by the creator |
| Banned phrases | Cliches, slang, claims, or language to avoid |
| Humor | Deadpan, dry, playful, sarcastic, none |
| Emotional range | How far the voice can move from calm to intense |
| Authority | How confident the creator should sound |
| CTA style | Binary, prediction, experience, correction, save, share |
| Caption style | Short, explanatory, question-led, source-led |
| Visual pattern | Talking head, maps, documents, screenshots, B-roll, etc. |
| Safety limits | Legal, privacy, minor, health, brand, or sponsor restrictions |

## Building the Profile

1. Read 5-10 approved examples when available.
2. Mark repeated sentence structures and transitions.
3. Separate topic voice from personal voice. A news segment may be calmer
   than an entertainment segment.
4. Record what the creator refuses to say.
5. Keep names, legal terms, and factual qualifiers out of stylistic rewriting.
6. Update the profile after feedback instead of creating contradictory rules.

## Application Order

Apply the voice profile in this order:

1. Facts and legal limits
2. Primary viewer and promise
3. Voice and tone
4. Hook and pacing
5. CTA and caption

Style never changes the truth, legal stage, attribution, or privacy boundary.

## Imitation Boundary

- Do not impersonate another living creator's signature voice without
  permission.
- Do not copy distinctive catchphrases, delivery, or identity markers from
  another creator.
- Use the profile to reflect the user's own approved writing and brand.
- When no profile exists, use natural US TikTok English and mark the voice as
  provisional.
