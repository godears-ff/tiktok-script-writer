# Production and Publishing

Use this reference for full deliverables, publishing preparation, testing,
analytics review, accessibility, corrections, and recurring content.

## Caption and Discovery

The caption should add context or a reason to engage. It should not simply
repeat the first spoken line.

Include:

- A first line that works before TikTok expands to "more"
- One or two searchable terms a viewer would naturally use
- A specific question only when it fits the content
- Source or update language for news and developing stories
- Hashtags only when they improve discovery or context

Avoid:

- Keyword stuffing
- Unrelated trending hashtags
- Claims that the video does not support
- Generic captions such as "You won't believe this"

## Sound-Off Readability

Many viewers watch without sound. The video should still make sense.

- Put the main conflict, result, or question in the first text card.
- Keep on-screen text short and readable.
- Do not duplicate every spoken word in the overlay.
- Use no more than two short lines in a normal text card.
- Give important text enough time to read.
- Use contrast and size that remain readable on mobile.
- Avoid flashing, rapid strobe effects, and dense text animation.
- Verify names, numbers, and legal terms in captions.

## Voiceover and Delivery Notes

When the user will record the script, add delivery guidance separately from
the spoken text:

- Pace: fast, moderate, or deliberate
- Pronunciation: names, places, brands, legal terms, and foreign words
- Pauses: where to let a fact land or create suspense
- Emphasis: which words carry the conflict
- Tone shifts: where the narrator becomes serious, skeptical, warm, or excited
- Breath points: natural places to avoid sounding rushed

Do not place stage directions inside the English spoken script.

## Test Plan

Test a hypothesis, not a promise. Change one primary variable at a time:

- Hook wording or visual
- Cover frame
- Caption first line
- Opening edit pace
- CTA

Record:

1. The variable being tested
2. The version identifiers
3. The primary signal
4. The secondary signal
5. The review window
6. The decision rule

Do not compare videos with materially different topics, audiences, or posting
conditions and treat the result as a clean experiment.

## Analytics Review

Use retention and interaction data as diagnostic evidence.

| Signal | What it may reveal |
| --- | --- |
| 3-second retention | Hook clarity or relevance problem |
| Early drop-off | Context begins too slowly or promise is unclear |
| Mid-video drop-off | Repetition, weak evidence, or unclear conflict |
| Rewatches | Strong reveal, dense value, or loop construction |
| Shares | Public value, identity value, or debate value |
| Saves | Reference value, timeline value, or future utility |
| Comments | Ambiguity, disagreement, missing context, or factual questions |
| Follows | Promise of consistently useful content |
| Negative feedback | Overpromising, misleading packaging, or poor audience fit |

Review at useful intervals such as one hour, 24 hours, and seven days when
data volume is sufficient. Do not overreact to tiny samples or treat a
correlation as proof.

## Recurring Series

A series should have:

- A clear premise
- A recognizable visual system
- A stable promise
- Episode numbering or another continuity cue
- A reason the next episode exists

Do not split one complete story into artificial parts. Each installment
should deliver a meaningful unit of value.

## Corrections and Updates

When a material fact changes:

1. Stop distribution of the outdated claim when possible.
2. Publish a correction or update with the previous wording and new wording.
3. Pin the correction when appropriate.
4. Update the source ledger and `As of` date.
5. In a new video, lead with the correction rather than burying it.

Never quietly delete an error without acknowledging it when the original
claim had meaningful reach or impact.

## Community Moderation

- Pin a useful source, correction, or context comment.
- Answer repeated factual questions with evidence.
- Do not amplify harassment, doxxing, or unsupported accusations.
- Hide or report replies that threaten, expose private information, or target
  protected characteristics.
- Separate legitimate criticism from abuse.
- For sensitive stories, disable or restrict replies when the comment section
  is creating meaningful harm.

## Disclosure and Rights

- Disclose paid partnerships, gifted products, affiliate links, and branded
  content as required by law and platform policy.
- Disclose realistic AI-generated or materially altered media when required.
- Use original, licensed, or platform-approved audio and footage.
- Do not imply that attribution alone clears copyright.
- Do not promise legal, medical, or financial outcomes.
- Check current rules before publishing gambling, firearms, medical,
  political, sexual, minor-related, or crisis content.
